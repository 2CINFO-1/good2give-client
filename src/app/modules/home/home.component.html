About Good2Give

Good2Give is a platform designed to connect supermarkets, restaurants, and other stakeholders to efficiently redistribute unsold food items, minimizing food waste. Our mission is to create sustainable food distribution networks that benefit communities and the environment.

We integrate artificial intelligence to enhance collection, sorting, and redistribution processes, ensuring optimal resource utilization and reducing environmental impact. Our comprehensive platform provides user management, delivery tracking, event organization, and food quality inspection.
