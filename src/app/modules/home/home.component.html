About Us

Good2Give is a non-profit organization dedicated to bridging the gap between those with excess resources and those in need. Our mission is to create a more sustainable and equitable community by efficiently redistributing essential goods.

Founded in 2023, we've helped thousands of individuals and families access essential items while reducing waste. Our team of dedicated volunteers works tirelessly to ensure that items reach those who need them most.
